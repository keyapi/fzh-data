1. **Blocking — unrelated SO overdelivery cancels outstanding demand.**  
   **Location:** `build_v0.py:516–518`.  
   `live_open = reconciled - dead` retains negative balances from other SOs. White has **26** outstanding but reports **20** after another SO’s −6; Natural-194 has **631** but reports **615**. Across ten SKUs, positive outstanding demand is **2,924**, versus published **2,017**: **907 units understated**.  
   **Minimal fix:** sum positive remaining quantities per live SO line; retain overdelivery separately unless an explicit reallocation proves it settles another obligation.

2. **Blocking — quantity subtraction does not establish supply deduplication.**  
   **Location:** `build_v0.py:486–498`, `546–558`.  
   Equal SKU/warehouse/quantity removes **281 PO units** without proving duplicate documents. `max(WO − PO, 0)` assumes maximum WO/PO overlap, while `max(transit − PO, 0)` assumes maximum transit/PO overlap. White’s WO20 disappears from B simply because PO73 exists; no linkage establishes that relationship. These are overlap scenarios, not verified “production not in PO” or “independent transit.”  
   **Minimal fix:** subtract only linked, allocated overlap quantities. Until links exist, publish explicit overlap/no-overlap bounds and label PO duplicate candidates as separate sensitivities.

3. **Major — lead-time demand is detached from the inventory timepoint and seasonal forecast path.**  
   **Location:** `build_v0.py:52–54`, `371–372`, `561–572`.  
   Inventory is dated **October 22**, forecasts cover full **October–December**, and every horizon uses their average monthly rate. Thus 60/90-day ROP does not integrate demand over its actual calendar window. SO/WO balances also come from October 20/21 without a reconciliation bridge to October 22.  
   **Minimal fix:** define the decision timestamp, integrate remaining forecast buckets over `[decision time, decision time + horizon]`, and reconcile intervening SO/WO movements or label their effects as separate scenarios.

4. **Major — monthly and cumulative “P90” use inconsistent uncertainty assumptions.**  
   **Location:** `build_v0.py:365–370`, `416–417`, `805`.  
   Monthly margins grow by √h, while cumulative margins grow by √3 under a stated independent-error assumption. White’s monthly margins are **126.6/179.1/219.4**; those independent variances imply a cumulative margin around **310.2**, not **219.4**. Moreover, observed cumulative coverage is **70%**, so 90% service is unvalidated.  
   **Minimal fix:** use consistent marginal/cumulative error models and calibrate using earlier rolling-origin errors. Record `target_service_level=0.90` separately from achieved coverage; label uncalibrated outputs as estimates.

5. **Minor — zero-demand cells are incorrectly excluded from metrics.**  
   **Location:** `build_v0.py:196–205`.  
   WAPE/bias should retain zero-actual cells’ forecast errors whenever total actual demand is positive; coverage should include those cells too. **Current published metrics are unaffected:** neither horizon excludes any of its 60 cells.  
   **Minimal fix:** retain all eligible cells; handle a zero aggregate denominator separately.
